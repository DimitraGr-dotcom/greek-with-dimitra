Lesson 3: The Plural (Ο Πληθυντικός) 🇬🇷

Transforming words from Singular (One) to Plural (Many)

How to form the Plural in Greek

Just like in English we add an "s" (apple -> apples), in Greek we change both the article and the ending of the noun. The rules depend on the gender!

🟦 Masculine (Αρσενικό)

Article changes from Ο to Οι. Ending changes from -ος / -ας / -ης to -οι / -ες.

Singular (1)

Ο φίλος (The friend)

Ο μαθητής (The student)

Ο πατέρας (The father)

Plural (2+)

Οι φίλοι (The friends)

Οι μαθητές (The students)

Οι πατέρες (The fathers)

🟥 Feminine (Θηλυκό)

Article changes from Η to Οι. Ending changes from -α / -η to -ες.

Singular (1)

Η γυναίκα (The woman)

Η φίλη (The friend)

Plural (2+)

Οι γυναίκες (The women)

Οι φίλες (The friends)

* Notice that Masculine and Feminine both use the plural article Οι!

🟩 Neuter (Ουδέτερο)

Article changes from Το to Τα. Ending changes from -ο to -α, and from -ι to -ια.

Singular (1)

Το βιβλίο (The book)

Το παιδί (The child)

Plural (2+)

Τα βιβλία (The books)

Τα παιδιά (The children)
